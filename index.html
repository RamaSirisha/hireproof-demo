Step iii · Privacy review

Nothing leaves until you approve it.

Check each redacted resume before it is shared with an AI service. Highlighted items are anything personal that may have slipped through.

Level of protection

Never share

Words hidden from every resume before sharing, such as a client's name or internal project codes. One per line.

Settings · AI access

Bring your own key.

Connect any OpenAI-compatible AI provider with your own key. Type the provider's name or paste your key, and the address fills in by itself. Add several in order of preference; if one reaches its usage limit, screening continues with the next.

Keys are off in this demo, and the AI results are pre-recorded. In the full version you connect your own key, such as a free Groq key.

Keys live only in this tab's memory and are sent only to the provider they belong to. Only approved, redacted text is shared. Some providers, especially on free plans, may use what you send to improve their services, so check their terms and your organisation's policy before using real candidate data.

Step v · Shortlist

Who to call, and what to ask.

Ranked by fit, then credibility, then score, then availability. Open a row for the evidence and two questions built from that candidate's own claims.

HireProof recommends; people decide. A low credibility rating means verify carefully, not reject.

Governance

Privacy you can explain.

How data moves, the controls in place, and a record of this session's actions, ready for your compliance team.

What leaves this device

Flow: Brief → Candidates → Privacy review → Screening → Shortlist. AI access is optional.

  1. Resume files — never. Read and redacted inside this tab.
  2. Job description — only when you click Draft criteria, to your AI provider. No candidate data.
  3. Redacted resumes — only after you approve them, and only when you run the AI assessment or copy them for a chat.
  4. Names and contacts — never shared. Kept in memory for your own exports.
  5. API keys — only to their own provider. Never saved.
  6. Page resources — document readers and fonts load from public libraries. No candidate data.

Session controls

A warning appears if you try to close the tab with candidates still loaded. Exported files are yours to store and delete under your organisation's retention policy.
